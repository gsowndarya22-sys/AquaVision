ABOUT AQUAVISION

Smart Water Management for Rayalaseema

AquaVision is a software-based smart irrigation management platform designed to support farmers in making informed irrigation decisions.

⚠️

The Problem

Farmers may apply water without considering current soil moisture or rainfall conditions, which can lead to unnecessary water use.

💡

Our Solution

AquaVision combines field information and weather information to provide a simple irrigation decision-support recommendation.

🌾

Why Rayalaseema?

The project focuses on Rayalaseema to demonstrate a region-specific approach to efficient irrigation and water management.

🚀

Future Scope

Future versions can integrate live weather APIs, improved crop models, real-time soil sensors and more advanced irrigation prediction.
